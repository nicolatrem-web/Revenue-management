Aquarium 

Maui Ocean Center - Voted one of Hawai‘i’s top rated family attractions. Explore the underwater world with over 60 exhibits of coral, fish, turtles, sharks and jellies. Visit the gift shop for memorable souvenirs. Maui Ocean center is a three-acre, state of the art aquarium and marine park.  Superb indoor exhibits and outdoor pools allow visitors and extraordinary look at the vast undersea world surround the islands, including large open ocean and game fish, tiger, gray and reef sharks, green sea turtles, stink rays, eels and jelly fish.  All the marine life in the facility is indigenous or endemic to the Hawaiian Islands.   

Highlights include:  The Living Reef, which features live coral displays and colorful tropical reef fish species; The Whale Discovery Center, a computerized arcade of interactive learning stations as well as life size models of humpback whales; Hawaiians & the sea, a tribute to the wisdom and culture of the Hawaiians, and their special connection with the sea; the Open Ocean exhibit, which features an acrylic tunnel where visitors stroll through a 750,000 gallon saltwater aquarium filled with nearly 2000 fishes, sharks, and underwater creatures.
